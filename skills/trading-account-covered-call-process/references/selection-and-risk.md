### Covered-call selection and risk-analysis phase

If the Covered-call screening rules are met, provide up to `${MAX_COVERED_CALL_CANDIDATES}` covered-call candidates. Expiration must be `${MIN_OPTION_DTE}` through `${MAX_OPTION_DTE}` calendar days from today inclusive.

For high-share-retention qualification, require absolute Robinhood call delta `<= ${MAX_COVERED_CALL_ABS_DELTA}`. Delta is the controlling contract-level sensitivity limit, not a probability of retaining the shares. Robinhood short-side probability of profit remains required reporting data but is not a share-retention probability or a separate covered-call qualification threshold. If delta or short-side probability of profit is unavailable, exclude the contract. Never calculate `probability of expiring out-of-the-money = ${RATIO_BASELINE} - absolute delta`, and never describe delta, probability of profit, or probability of touch as a probability of retaining the shares.

Robinhood-provided probability of expiring out-of-the-money is optional reporting data for covered calls because the current official Robinhood MCP option quote does not expose it. When available, report it separately. Its absence does not disqualify the contract or stop a Required Trade Preview. High-share-retention remains a delta-, event-, basis-, and empirically calibrated objective rather than a broker-modeled share-retention guarantee.

High-share-retention qualification also requires that no verified earnings release, ex-dividend date, or other material event occur from the decision timestamp through expiration, inclusive. A current-conversation event-risk exception may permit evaluation under the general event-exception policy, but the candidate must then be labeled `EVENT-RISK EXCEPTION - HIGH-SHARE-RETENTION TARGET NOT APPLICABLE`; do not describe any probability metric or delta as a probability of retaining the shares.

Show the estimated option premium at the proposed rounded limit and apply the same fee-reliability rule defined in CSP Selection. Use fee `${ZERO_VALUE}` only when Robinhood explicitly confirms that no fee applies; if a required fee cannot be retrieved or reliably estimated, exclude the contract. Calculate the return fields as:

```text
current_covering_share_value = fresh Robinhood regular-session underlying price * ${STANDARD_OPTION_CONTRACT_SHARES}
holding_period_net_ROC = (gross premium at the proposed rounded limit - estimated fees) / current_covering_share_value
normalized_net_ROC = holding_period_net_ROC * ${COVERED_CALL_ROC_NORMALIZATION_DAYS} / days to expiration
```

Require `normalized_net_ROC >= ${MIN_COVERED_CALL_NORMALIZED_NET_ROC}`. Report current covering-share value as the denominator, the underlying quote timestamp, holding-period net ROC, and normalized net ROC. When useful, calculate simple annualized return as `holding_period_net_ROC * ${DAYS_PER_YEAR} / days to expiration`; label it as an estimate and do not compound it. Apply the same quote-freshness, nonzero-bid, option-volume, open-interest, and spread-width requirements used for CSPs. If no contract qualifies, hold the shares until the SHORT CALL PROCESS criteria are met and record `NO TRADE`.


The preference is to retain the shares, but a covered call cannot guarantee that outcome. A short equity call may be assigned before expiration, including when it is out-of-the-money. Before every covered-call order, explicitly state that assignment would sell `${STANDARD_OPTION_CONTRACT_SHARES}` shares per contract at the strike price and ask me to acknowledge that call-away risk as part of the trade confirmation. If retaining the shares is mandatory, do not propose or submit a covered call.
